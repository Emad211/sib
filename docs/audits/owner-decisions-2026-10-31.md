# Owner decisions, 2026-10-31, for the remaining shift-app work

Accepted by the owner. Record as ADR-022 in docs/03_DECISIONS.md when building. Where these differ from an older doc, these win.

1. Vendor goods join main-store stock automatically, on the storekeeper's count at delivery. The storekeeper counts the main store for the initial count.
2. The weekly count covers all main-store items; one counted item does not close it. The accountant's sample count: 5 random items, monthly.
3. Cash deposit to the bank: recorded by the accountant, receipt photo optional.
4. Follow-up path reward: until the follow-up system exists, the manager confirms it by hand monthly.
5. Duplicate mobile: accounting only warns; a reward for a given mobile is earned once. Random call sample on the manager card.
6. Invoice photo is mandatory when receiving goods; payment receipt photo is optional.
7. Team goal: one monthly number the manager sets beside reward rates. Learning mode: the first 7 days after the initial count, discrepancies are shown to the manager as learning only.

Settled by the owner's logic document (master's reading):
8. Trolley checklist only in the nursing flow; general space and doctor's room with reception.
9. Initial count: each person in their own flow on their own phone, and the baseline is recorded under that person's name; the manager keeps the tool as a fallback.
10. The accountant has no right to count cash or sign a handover: remove from the role's grants.
11. Sugar and pressure thresholds, oxygen pressure threshold, and cash float are settings the manager enters. With no number set, the app makes no suggestion. With a number set, above-threshold readings suggest opening the follow-up path with one button (decision 17 of the logic document). This supersedes the "no alert" part of ADR-020.

Also from the owner: access follows handover (ADR-021). The clinic's name does not appear in the app.
